# Digital Marketing Task Frameworks

This is a specialized, task-level companion to the six cross-functional AI marketing frameworks at the repository root. It contains 92 distinct working frameworks across nine disciplines: SEO (12), PPC (10), social media (10), content (10), email (10), influencer (10), video advertising (10), conversion optimization (10), and marketing automation (10).

## What the count means

The source review processed 500 eligible pattern IDs. Those inputs were consolidated into 92 distinct framework deliverables: 88 newly authored assets and four extensions rewritten to work as standalone assets. The other 408 eligible patterns were merged into those deliverables; they are not 408 additional frameworks. The wider source screening also recorded duplicates, deferred items, and out-of-scope items. See the catalog and source notes for the audit trail.

## Use responsibly

These are general working aids, not legal, financial, privacy, platform-policy, or compliance determinations. Examples may be synthetic and must not be represented as client results. Validate time-sensitive platform behavior and local rules against current primary sources; use merchant or campaign data instead of generic benchmark claims. Assign a qualified human reviewer before acting. `READY_FOR_REVIEW` in source files is a workflow label, not a claim of production, legal, or regulatory approval.

The four video-related extensions are self-contained. Their optional integration note does not depend on a separate `video-prompt-architect` skill.
