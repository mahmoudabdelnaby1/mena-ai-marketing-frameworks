# FW-091 — Worked Example

**Illustrative evaluation plan only.** A team needs to draft bilingual product descriptions and classify incoming feedback.

The team creates separate synthetic evaluation sets for writing and classification, lists language and privacy requirements, checks current provider catalogs, and compares candidate outputs against human-reviewed criteria. It records latency and price assumptions from current provider sources and defines a human fallback for uncertain classifications. The team sets a re-check date rather than treating the selection as permanent.
