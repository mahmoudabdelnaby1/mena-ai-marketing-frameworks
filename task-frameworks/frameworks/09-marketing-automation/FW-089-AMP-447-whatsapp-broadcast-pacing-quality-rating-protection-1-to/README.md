# `FW-089` — WhatsApp Broadcast Quality & Consent Review
## مراجعة جودة الرسائل التسويقية والموافقة عبر WhatsApp

- **One-Sentence Promise (EN)**: Plan a WhatsApp marketing send around documented permission, current product limits, customer preferences, and an accountable pause-and-review process.
- **الوعد التطبيقي في جملة واحدة (AR)**: نظّم إرسال الرسائل التسويقية عبر واتساب استنادًا إلى موافقة موثقة وحدود المنتج الحالية وتفضيلات العملاء وآلية إيقاف ومراجعة واضحة.
- **Selected Artifact Type**: `OPERATING_FRAMEWORK`
- **Intended Audience**: CRM, lifecycle marketing, and marketing-operations teams.
- **Markets & Languages**: General operating guidance; verify each market separately.

## When to Use This Asset
Use before a planned marketing send, when refreshing an audience, or after a change in delivery, opt-out, or account-quality signals.

## Quick Start
1. Confirm the source of permission, purpose, audience, and current WhatsApp policy for the exact account and message type.
2. Test the message, template status, preference and suppression flow, recipient data, and operational owner with a small approved review group.
3. Define account-specific stop conditions, monitor delivery and customer feedback, and record who can pause or resume the send.

## Boundaries
A quick-reply button, send schedule, audience segment, or template approval does not guarantee quality, delivery, or compliance. Do not message people without valid permission. Check current official product guidance and applicable market requirements before use.
