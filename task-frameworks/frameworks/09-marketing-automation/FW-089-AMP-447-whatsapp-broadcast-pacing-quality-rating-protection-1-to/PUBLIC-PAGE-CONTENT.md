# WhatsApp Broadcast Quality & Consent Review
## <span dir="rtl">مراجعة جودة الرسائل التسويقية والموافقة عبر WhatsApp</span>

Plan a WhatsApp marketing send around documented permission, current product limits, customer preferences, and an accountable pause-and-review process.

## When to use it
Use before a planned marketing send, after changing the audience or message, or when delivery and customer-feedback signals change.

## Prepare
- The message, purpose, audience, language, market, and intended timing.
- The permission source and its date, plus audience-data freshness.
- Current account limits, message/template status, available monitoring signals, and applicable market guidance.
- The suppression path and the people responsible for monitoring, pausing, and approving a restart.

## Working sequence
1. Confirm that each recipient has documented permission for the intended marketing use.
2. Check current official WhatsApp guidance and the active account interface for the relevant message type and limits.
3. Remove stale, suppressed, invalid, or out-of-scope contacts; document how the audience was selected.
4. Test the message and verify that customer opt-out requests reach the suppression system across connected tools.
5. Set stop conditions using the account's own baseline and current policy. Name the person who can pause the send.
6. Send only within current account limits. Monitor account notices, delivery issues, opt-outs, and customer feedback.
7. If a stop condition is reached, pause, investigate, record the response, and obtain human approval before resuming.

## Output
A documented send plan, tested permission and suppression handling, an assigned monitor and approver, account-specific stop conditions, and a decision record.

## Review boundary
A template approval, quick-reply button, audience segment, or sending schedule does not guarantee message quality, delivery, or compliance. Confirm current platform behavior and market-specific requirements before acting. This is general operational guidance, not legal advice.
