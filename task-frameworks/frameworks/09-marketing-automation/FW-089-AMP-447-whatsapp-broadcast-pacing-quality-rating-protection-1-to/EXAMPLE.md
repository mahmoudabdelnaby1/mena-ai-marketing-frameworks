# FW-089 — Worked Example

**Illustrative scenario only; not a client result.** A retailer plans a bilingual seasonal WhatsApp message to customers who opted in during checkout.

1. The CRM owner exports only records with a dated permission source and removes globally suppressed contacts.
2. The team checks the active account's template status, applicable send limits, current Meta policy, and the relevant local requirements.
3. A test confirms that the chosen preference route creates a suppression record shared with the CRM and messaging provider.
4. The marketing lead approves an initial send plan based on the account's own baseline and available capacity. A named operator monitors delivery, customer feedback, and account notices.
5. The team pauses if a pre-agreed account or policy signal is reached, investigates, and records the decision before any restart.

The example intentionally includes no benchmark, guaranteed outcome, universal send rate, or fixed restriction duration.
