# Evidence Register & Source Boundaries — `FW-089`

This framework is an operational checklist, not a claim that any specific tactic prevents account restrictions or guarantees delivery.

| Source | What to verify | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [WhatsApp Business Platform policy enforcement](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/policy-enforcement) | Current policy, account notices, and enforcement details for the active account. | Do not infer a universal threshold, trigger, or restriction duration. |
| [WhatsApp marketing templates](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/marketing-templates) | Current requirements, supported template components, and account-specific template status. | Do not assume a particular opt-out control is available or required for every message type; verify the active product and policy. |

## Human review
Before sending, the accountable team must verify recipient permission, purpose, market-specific obligations, suppression behavior, current account limits, and a safe pause/review path. Record the source and date of each check.
