# MENA Social Channel Mix Validation Aid

Compare social-channel options for a defined market, audience, and business goal using current account data and clearly labeled external evidence. The worksheet helps teams document unknowns, check operational readiness, and plan a bounded test with a business-side measure.

It does not prescribe a universal channel ranking, budget split, market reach figure, or expected result. Platform-reported reach is not the same as unique people or confirmed business outcomes.

**Before use:** record the data source, date range, audience definition, attribution window, assumptions, and decision owner. Verify current platform details with official documentation.
