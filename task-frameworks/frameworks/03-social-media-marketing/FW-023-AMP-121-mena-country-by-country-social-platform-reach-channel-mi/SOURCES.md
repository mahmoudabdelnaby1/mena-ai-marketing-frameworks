# Sources and evidence boundaries

This aid is a documentation and decision workflow. It makes no numeric claim about current platform reach, channel rankings, budget allocation, or expected performance.

## Use evidence in this order

1. Current ad-account and analytics exports for the defined audience and date range.
2. Business records used to confirm the chosen outcome, such as qualified leads, orders, or fulfilled sales.
3. Current official platform documentation for availability, formats, targeting, and policy.
4. Dated, method-described external research as directional context only.

## Source register references

The repository-level [`SOURCES.md`](../../../SOURCES.md) lists external references and their support boundaries. In particular, external audience or reach estimates must not be treated as unique-person counts, a representative estimate for every segment, or evidence of conversion performance.

Re-check changing platform details at the time of use. Record source name, URL, date accessed, definition, and limitation alongside any estimate used in a specific decision.
