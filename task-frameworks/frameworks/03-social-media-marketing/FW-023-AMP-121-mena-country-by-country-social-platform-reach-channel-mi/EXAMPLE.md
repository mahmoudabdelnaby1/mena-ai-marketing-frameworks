# Worked example — channel test decision

**Illustrative scenario only. No client results or market benchmarks are claimed.**

## Brief

A retailer active in one market is considering a second market. The team has campaign reports from the current market but no comparable account data for the new one.

## Review

1. Keep current-market results labeled as observed results for that account and period.
2. List candidate channels for the new market, then document audience-fit evidence and its date for each.
3. Mark channels without current evidence as hypotheses; do not assign them a presumed share based on population or another market's reports.
4. Confirm destination, tracking, creative, customer response, and fulfillment readiness before spending.
5. Propose a bounded test with a budget owner, primary business metric, guardrails, and review date.

## Decision record

| Candidate | Evidence status | Main unknown | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Channel A | Current account evidence in the original market only | Audience fit in the new market | Validate audience and placement availability; test only if ready |
| Channel B | No comparable account evidence | Whether creative and funnel can support it | Prepare a small, measurable test or defer |
| Channel C | External estimate available, methodology not yet checked | Relevance to the target audience | Verify publisher, date, and definition before using |

This example demonstrates the decision process; it does not prescribe a platform mix, budget percentage, or expected outcome.
