# FW-023 — MENA Social Channel Mix Validation Aid

**Decision aid · Review draft · 2026-10-25**

## What this helps decide

Which social channels merit a measured test for a defined audience, offer, market, and business objective. It does not provide a universal ranking or pre-set percentage split.

## Input worksheet

| Input | Record before deciding |
|---|---|
| Business outcome | One primary outcome and its business-side confirmation source |
| Market and audience | Country, relevant audience definition, language, and exclusions |
| Current evidence | Account, dates, spend, reach, frequency, conversions, attribution window, and source |
| Business constraints | Creative capacity, stock or service coverage, response time, and landing-page readiness |
| Open questions | Missing or conflicting evidence; do not replace with assumed market averages |

## Compare candidate channels

Score each criterion as **supported by current evidence**, **needs a test**, or **not ready**. Keep the underlying evidence beside each judgment.

| Criterion | Review question |
|---|---|
| Audience fit | Is there current evidence that the intended audience can be reached on this channel? |
| Intent and outcome | Can the channel support the chosen customer action and its measurement? |
| Creative fit | Can the team produce and review suitable assets for the placement? |
| Economics | Are costs and downstream outcomes comparable over the same period and definition? |
| Readiness | Are targeting, destination, tracking, consent, and fulfillment ready? |

## Test plan

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| Hypothesis | What will this channel test that the current plan cannot answer? |
| Test boundary | Audience, placement, creative, dates, and budget source |
| Primary measure | One outcome metric plus the business system used to confirm it |
| Guardrails | Quality, frequency, cost, customer experience, and policy checks relevant to the brief |
| Review date | Date and owner for the decision |
| Next action | Continue, revise, expand cautiously, or stop—with the evidence recorded |

## Interpretation rules

- Keep platform-reported reach separate from unique people and business outcomes.
- Do not compare results with different attribution windows, conversion definitions, or date ranges as if equivalent.
- A market estimate is directional evidence only; cite its date and methodology and confirm fit to the audience.
- Reassess platform access, ad formats, and policies against current official documentation.
- Treat an untested channel allocation as a hypothesis, not a proven recommendation.

## Deliverable

A market-specific comparison and test brief with evidence, assumptions, limits, owner, and a review date. The worksheet itself does not predict reach, sales, or return.
