# MENA Social Channel Mix Validation Aid

## أداة التحقق من مزيج قنوات التواصل الاجتماعي في أسواق المنطقة

- **Asset ID**: `FW-023`
- **Type**: Decision aid
- **Status**: Review draft
- **Markets**: Egypt and GCC markets, selected per brief

### Purpose

Use this worksheet to compare channel options for a specific audience and business goal. It does not prescribe a universal platform ranking, reach estimate, or budget split. Platform availability, audience composition, costs, and performance change by market, account, and period.

### Use it when

Planning a new market test, reviewing channel concentration, or deciding whether to extend a campaign to another platform.

### Prepare

- Target market, audience, offer, objective, and conversion event.
- Recent account-level reach, spend, frequency, and outcome data, with date ranges and attribution definitions.
- Creative capacity, landing-page readiness, fulfillment constraints, and platform eligibility.
- Any external audience or market evidence, with publisher, date, methodology, and limitations.

### Working steps

1. Separate observed account results from external market estimates and assumptions.
2. Compare candidate channels against audience fit, measurable intent, creative fit, cost, and operational readiness.
3. Record unknowns instead of filling them with generic market percentages.
4. Design a bounded test with a stated hypothesis, budget source, success metric, guardrail, and review date.
5. Reconcile platform reporting with analytics and business records before reallocating spend.

### Limits

Do not treat ad-reach estimates as unique people, infer sales from reach, or copy one market's channel mix into another. Validate changing platform and market details with current sources. This is a planning aid, not a forecast or performance guarantee.
