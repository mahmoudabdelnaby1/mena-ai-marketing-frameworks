# End-to-End Worked Example & Failure Recovery — `FW-029` (`AMP-127`)

- **Framework**: Saudi Marketing Team Planning & Nitaqat Verification Decision Aid
- **العنوان بالعربية**: أداة تخطيط فريق التسويق والتحقق من متطلبات نطاقات في السعودية
- **Selected Artifact Type**: `DECISION_AID`
- **Disclaimer**: *All scenarios, brand names, SKU codes, and metrics below are original, realistic illustrative examples for MENA practitioner training and are never represented as verified client outcomes.*

## 1. Realistic Sample Brief / Input (`المدخلات وموجز الحالة العملية`)

- **Company**: Riyadh Series-A FinTech & E-Commerce Platform planning to grow its in-house marketing team from 2 to 6 employees in Q4 2026.
- **Draft AI Org Chart**: Suggested hiring 1 Saudi PR Manager and 5 expat specialists in Riyadh on work visas (`16.7%` localization), which would immediately fail MHRSD's النسبة المحسوبة وفق الأداة الرسمية الحالية localization requirements on Qiwa.

## 2. Initial Generated / Completed Output (`المخرج الأولي عند تطبيق الأداة`)

- **Compliant 6-Person Saudi Marketing Org Structure (`66.7%` Saudization — Exceeds النسبة المحسوبة وفق الأداة الرسمية الحالية Mandate)**:
  - **4 Saudi Nationals (`>= SAR 5,500+` GOSI wage)**:
    1. Head of Marketing & Brand Strategy (Riyadh)
    2. Senior Saudi Content & Copywriting Lead (Owns Khaleeji voice & `AMP-177` QA)
    3. Social Media & Creator Partnerships Lead (Owns Snapchat/X & `Mawthooq` compliance `AMP-281`)
    4. Growth & CRM Lifecycle Specialist (Owns WhatsApp/Email retention & PDPL compliance `AMP-232`)
  - **2 Expat Specialists**:
    5. Senior Performance Marketing Manager (Google/Meta/Snap Paid Media & CAPI `AMP-061`..`AMP-068`)
    6. Technical SEO & Marketing Analytics Lead (GA4/BigQuery/Schema `AMP-001`..`AMP-012`, `AMP-443`)

## 3. Human Review & Localization/Technical Edits (`المراجعة البشرية والتعديلات الرقابية والتقنية`)

1. **Qiwa Occupation Code Alignment**: Verified that all 4 Saudi hires are registered under the exact MHRSD-designated marketing occupation codes in GOSI/Qiwa with registered wages comfortably above the `SAR 5,500` floor.
2. **AI Productivity Layer**: Equipped the 6-person team with automated JSON schema workflows (`AMP-445`) and bilingual catalog templates (`AMP-005`/`AMP-011`) so 6 staff cover the output of a traditional 12-person department.

## 4. Final Reviewed Example Outcome (`النتيجة النهائية الجاهزة للتنفيذ`)

- Approved 6-person KSA marketing org chart (`66.7%` Saudization) compliant with MHRSD's 19 April 2026 decision, with clear role division between cultural/strategic ownership and technical MarTech execution.

## 5. What Can Go Wrong & Specific Correction / Fallback (`حالة الفشل المحتملة وخطة الإنقاذ والبديل`)

- **Common Failure Mode (`ما الذي قد يسير بشكل خاطئ؟`)**:
  An HR/operations manager hires 2 Saudi junior interns at `SAR 4,000/month` hoping they count toward the النسبة المحسوبة وفق الأداة الرسمية الحالية marketing localization quota; because their GOSI wage is below the `SAR 5,500` minimum threshold, Qiwa counts them as `0` (or fractional) toward the specialized marketing localization ratio, blocking visa renewals.
- **Specific Correction & Non-AI / Platform Fallback (`الإصلاح المحدد والبديل الآمن`)**:
  Always verify the current role-specific wage conditions, if applicable and occupation code list in the official MHRSD Procedural Guide (`الدليل الإجرائي لتوطين مهن التسويق`) on `hrsd.gov.sa` / `qiwa.sa` before issuing offer letters.
