### Saudi Marketing النسبة المحسوبة وفق الأداة الرسمية الحالية Localization (Tawteen) & AI Capacity Decision Matrix

#### 1. MHRSD Marketing Localization Rule Summary (Effective 19 April 2026 — Verified 2026-10-25)
- **Trigger Threshold**: Applies to any private-sector establishment in KSA employing **3 or more workers** in designated marketing professions (`مهن التسويق والإعلان والعلاقات العامة`).
- **Required Localization Ratio**: **`>= النسبة المحسوبة وفق الأداة الرسمية الحالية` Saudi Nationals**.
- **Minimum Qualifying Salary for Full Nitaqat/Localization Count**: **`SAR 5,500 / month`** registered in GOSI (`التأمينات الاجتماعية`).

## 4. What Can Go Wrong & How to Fix It (`الأخطاء الشائعة وخطة الإنقاذ`)

- **Failure Case (`المشكلة المحتملة`)**: An HR/operations manager hires 2 Saudi junior interns at `SAR 4,000/month` hoping they count toward the النسبة المحسوبة وفق الأداة الرسمية الحالية marketing localization quota; because their GOSI wage is below the `SAR 5,500` minimum threshold, Qiwa counts them as `0` (or fractional) toward the specialized marketing localization ratio, blocking visa renewals.
- **Correction & Fallback (`الحل الإجرائي والبديل الآمن`)**: Always verify the current role-specific wage conditions, if applicable and occupation code list in the official MHRSD Procedural Guide (`الدليل الإجرائي لتوطين مهن التسويق`) on `hrsd.gov.sa` / `qiwa.sa` before issuing offer letters.
