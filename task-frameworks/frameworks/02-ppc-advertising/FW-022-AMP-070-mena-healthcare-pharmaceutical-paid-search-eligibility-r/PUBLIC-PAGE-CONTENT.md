- [ ] **Check 1 — Strip Prohibited Drug Brand Names from Ad Copy**: Even when advertising a licensed dermatology clinic in Riyadh or Dubai, including prescription toxin/filler brand names in Google Ad headlines triggers automated `Healthcare and medicines: Prescription drug terms` disapproval. Advertise the **licensed clinical consultation (`استشارة جلدية وتجميلية بترخيص وزارة الصحة رقم ...`)** instead.
- [ ] **Check 2 — Display License Badge in Hero Section**: Place the official `MOH / SFDA / MOHAP / DHA / EDA` permit number in plain HTML text at the top of the landing page so both human reviewers and automated policy crawlers verify it immediately.

## 3. Complete End-to-End Worked Example (`مثال تطبيقي متكامل من المدخلات حتى المراجعة البشرية والنتيجة النهائية`)

*(Illustrative scenario — original example for training, never represented as a verified client outcome)*

### Step A: Sample Brief / Input (`موجز المدخلات`)
- **Advertiser**: Multi-branch Dermatology & Aesthetic Clinic in Riyadh and Dubai.
- **Problem**: Agency tried to run Google Search ads with headlines containing prescription injectable brand names and pitched 'Google AI Mode Pharma Ads' for a new hair-loss treatment; 18 of 20 ads were disapproved for `Restricted drug terms` and missing DHA/MOH permit disclosures.

### Step B: Initial Output (`المخرج الأولي`)
- **Compliant Routing & Copy Restructure**:
  - Removed prescription drug brand names from Google Search headlines and pinned the clinic's official service category (`عيادة جلدية وليزر وعناية بالشعر`).
  - Added mandatory license footer pin:
    - Riyadh Campaign: `"ترخيص وزارة الصحة السعودية رقم: [MOH-XXXX]"`
    - Dubai Campaign: `"تصريح هيئة الصحة بدبي رقم: [DHA-AD-XXXX]"`

### Step C: Human Review & Edits (`التدقيق البشري والتعديلات`)
1. **Landing Page Audit (`AMP-176`)**: Replaced `"علاج نهائي ومضمون 100% لتساقط الشعر"` on the landing page with `"جلسات عناية متخصصة بفروة الرأس تحت إشراف أطباء جلدية مرخصين"`.
2. **Expectation Alignment**: Clarified in the media plan that Google AI Mode pharma ad inventory is not enabled for Arab jurisdictions, shifting budget to standard High-Intent Arabic/English Search + Google Maps Local Pack.

### Step D: Final Reviewed Example Outcome (`النتيجة النهائية المعتمدة`)
- `100%` of clinic Search ads approved in Riyadh and Dubai with visible `MOH` and `DHA` permit numbers and zero restricted-drug policy violations.

## 4. What Can Go Wrong & How to Fix It (`الأخطاء الشائعة وخطة الإنقاذ`)

- **Failure Case (`المشكلة المحتملة`)**: A copywriter uses leetspeak or spacing tricks (`بـ.ـوتـ.ـوكس` or `B.o.t.o.x`) to bypass Google Ads' automated prescription drug filter; Google's policy system flags the account for `Circumventing Systems`, suspending the entire ad account permanently.
- **Correction & Fallback (`الحل الإجرائي والبديل الآمن`)**: Never use obfuscation or character insertion to bypass medical ad filters (`Circumventing Systems` is a fatal account ban); remove restricted drug terms completely and apply for official Google Healthcare certification where eligible.

## 6. How to Measure Success (`خطة القياس والضوابط`)

- **Primary Metric**: Ad Approval Rate (`100%` approved without `Restricted drug terms` flags) and Cost per Booked Clinic Appointment.
- **Baseline**: Pre-fix ad disapproval rate.
- **Observation Window**: Checked at campaign launch and after every ad copy update.
- **Interpretation Boundary**: This decision aid routes ads to compliant formats; formal medical advertising approval always requires an active permit from KSA MOH/SFDA, UAE MOHAP/DHA/DOH, or Egypt MOH/EDA.
