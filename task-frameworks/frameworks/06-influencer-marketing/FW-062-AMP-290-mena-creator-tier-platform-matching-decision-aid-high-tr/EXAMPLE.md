# End-to-End Worked Example & Failure Recovery — `FW-062` (`AMP-290`)

- **Framework**: MENA Creator Tier & Platform Matching Decision Aid (High-Trust B2B / FinTech / Real Estate vs. Impulse B2C)
- **العنوان بالعربية**: أداة القرار لاختيار فئة المؤثر والمنصة المناسبة (القطاعات عالية الثقة `B2B / التقنية المالية / العقار` مقابل الشراء الاندفاعي `B2C`)
- **Selected Artifact Type**: `DECISION_AID`
- **Disclaimer**: *All scenarios, brand names, SKU codes, and metrics below are original, realistic illustrative examples for MENA practitioner training and are never represented as verified client outcomes.*

## 1. Realistic Sample Brief / Input (`المدخلات وموجز الحالة العملية`)

- **Client**: Riyadh & Cairo B2B Point-of-Sale (POS) & Inventory SaaS for Restaurants and Retail Stores (`Annual Plan: 4,800 SAR / 24,000 EGP`).
- **Previous Mistake**: Spent `SAR 65,000` on 2 celebrity family vloggers on Snapchat. Result: `1.1M` views, `3,400` app downloads by consumers thinking it was a food delivery app, and `0` restaurant-owner subscriptions.

## 2. Initial Generated / Completed Output (`المخرج الأولي عند تطبيق الأداة`)

- **Re-Aligned Creator Tier & Channel Plan (`SAR 65,000` Budget)**:
  1. **Booked 6 F&B / Retail Operations Consultants & Cafe Founders** on **LinkedIn, X (KSA), and YouTube/Instagram (Egypt & KSA)** (`12k–65k` followers each, audience `70%+` business owners/managers).
  2. **Content Format**: 2-minute live walkthrough inside a real Riyadh/Cairo cafe showing how the POS handles ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing (`فاتورة`) and food-cost inventory waste.

## 3. Human Review & Localization/Technical Edits (`المراجعة البشرية والتعديلات الرقابية والتقنية`)

1. **Added B2B Lead Qualification Gate (`AMP-126` & `AMP-446`)**: Routed creator links to a dedicated B2B demo page requiring `نوع النشاط التجاري (مطعم / كافيه / متجر تجزئة)` and `عدد الفروع` before scheduling a sales call.
2. **Contracted Usage Rights for LinkedIn/Meta Paid Boosting (`AMP-284`)**: Secured 90-day paid amplification rights on the 6 founder walkthrough videos.

## 4. Final Reviewed Example Outcome (`النتيجة النهائية الجاهزة للتنفيذ`)

- `6` domain-credible B2B creator walkthroughs generated `184` verified restaurant/retail owner demo bookings (`Cost per Qualified Demo: 353 SAR`), compared to `0` from the celebrity vlogger campaign.

## 5. What Can Go Wrong & Specific Correction / Fallback (`حالة الفشل المحتملة وخطة الإنقاذ والبديل`)

- **Common Failure Mode (`ما الذي قد يسير بشكل خاطئ؟`)**:
  A real estate developer in Riyadh pays a lifestyle influencer to post an ad for an off-plan residential project without displaying a valid REGA `Fal` (`رخصة فال`) advertisement license number; the Real Estate General Authority (`الهيئة العامة للعقار`) flags the ad for unlicensed real estate marketing.
- **Specific Correction & Non-AI / Platform Fallback (`الإصلاح المحدد والبديل الآمن`)**:
  For Real Estate in KSA (REGA `Fal`), Real Estate in Dubai (RERA `Trakheesi`), Healthcare (MOH/SFDA/DHA/EDA), and FinTech (SAMA/CMA/FRA), **verify both the media permit (`Mawthooq`/`Media Council`) AND the sector regulator's specific advertising permit** before briefing any creator.
