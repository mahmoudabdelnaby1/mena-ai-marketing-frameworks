## 3. Complete End-to-End Worked Example (`مثال تطبيقي متكامل من المدخلات حتى المراجعة البشرية والنتيجة النهائية`)

*(Illustrative scenario — original example for training, never represented as a verified client outcome)*

### Step A: Sample Brief / Input (`موجز المدخلات`)
- **Client**: Riyadh & Cairo B2B Point-of-Sale (POS) & Inventory SaaS for Restaurants and Retail Stores (`Annual Plan: 4,800 SAR / 24,000 EGP`).
- **Previous Mistake**: Spent `SAR 65,000` on 2 celebrity family vloggers on Snapchat. Result: `1.1M` views, `3,400` app downloads by consumers thinking it was a food delivery app, and `0` restaurant-owner subscriptions.

### Step B: Initial Output (`المخرج الأولي`)
- **Re-Aligned Creator Tier & Channel Plan (`SAR 65,000` Budget)**:
  1. **Booked 6 F&B / Retail Operations Consultants & Cafe Founders** on **LinkedIn, X (KSA), and YouTube/Instagram (Egypt & KSA)** (`12k–65k` followers each, audience `70%+` business owners/managers).
  2. **Content Format**: 2-minute live walkthrough inside a real Riyadh/Cairo cafe showing how the POS handles ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing (`فاتورة`) and food-cost inventory waste.

### Step C: Human Review & Edits (`التدقيق البشري والتعديلات`)
1. **Added B2B Lead Qualification Gate (`AMP-126` & `AMP-446`)**: Routed creator links to a dedicated B2B demo page requiring `نوع النشاط التجاري (مطعم / كافيه / متجر تجزئة)` and `عدد الفروع` before scheduling a sales call.
2. **Contracted Usage Rights for LinkedIn/Meta Paid Boosting (`AMP-284`)**: Secured 90-day paid amplification rights on the 6 founder walkthrough videos.

### Step D: Final Reviewed Example Outcome (`النتيجة النهائية المعتمدة`)
- `6` domain-credible B2B creator walkthroughs generated `184` verified restaurant/retail owner demo bookings (`Cost per Qualified Demo: 353 SAR`), compared to `0` from the celebrity vlogger campaign.

## 4. What Can Go Wrong & How to Fix It (`الأخطاء الشائعة وخطة الإنقاذ`)

- **Failure Case (`المشكلة المحتملة`)**: A real estate developer in Riyadh pays a lifestyle influencer to post an ad for an off-plan residential project without displaying a valid REGA `Fal` (`رخصة فال`) advertisement license number; the Real Estate General Authority (`الهيئة العامة للعقار`) flags the ad for unlicensed real estate marketing.
- **Correction & Fallback (`الحل الإجرائي والبديل الآمن`)**: For Real Estate in KSA (REGA `Fal`), Real Estate in Dubai (RERA `Trakheesi`), Healthcare (MOH/SFDA/DHA/EDA), and FinTech (SAMA/CMA/FRA), **verify both the media permit (`Mawthooq`/`Media Council`) AND the sector regulator's specific advertising permit** before briefing any creator.

## 6. How to Measure Success (`خطة القياس والضوابط`)

- **Primary Metric**: Cost per Sales-Qualified Lead (`Cost / SQL` or `Cost / Verified Demo`) rather than raw video views or CPM.
- **Baseline**: Prior campaign Cost per SQL across paid social and influencer channels.
- **Observation Window**: 28 days post-publication (to account for B2B/high-ticket sales cycle).
- **Interpretation Boundary**: Matching creator tier to vertical intent delivers qualified prospects, while sales team follow-up speed (`<15 min`) determines closed-won revenue.
