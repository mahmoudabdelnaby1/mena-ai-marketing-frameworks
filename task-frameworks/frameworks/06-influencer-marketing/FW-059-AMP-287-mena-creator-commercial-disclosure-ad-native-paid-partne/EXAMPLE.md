# End-to-End Worked Example & Failure Recovery — `FW-059` (`AMP-287`)

- **Framework**: MENA Creator Commercial Disclosure (`#إعلان` / `#Ad`) & Native Paid-Partnership Tagging Checklist
- **العنوان بالعربية**: قائمة فحص الإفصاح الإعلاني الإلزامي للمؤثرين (`#إعلان` / `#Ad` ووسم الشراكة المدفوعة) في قصص وفيديوهات التواصل الاجتماعي
- **Selected Artifact Type**: `CHECKLIST`
- **Disclaimer**: *All scenarios, brand names, SKU codes, and metrics below are original, realistic illustrative examples for MENA practitioner training and are never represented as verified client outcomes.*

## 1. Realistic Sample Brief / Input (`المدخلات وموجز الحالة العملية`)

- **Campaign**: Riyadh & Dubai Electronics Brand running a 5-Snap story sequence with a tech creator.
- **Draft Submitted**: Creator put `#إعلان` in tiny translucent grey letters on Snap #1 only (right under the Snapchat close `'X'` button) and left Snaps #2–#5 completely unlabeled.

## 2. Initial Generated / Completed Output (`المخرج الأولي عند تطبيق الأداة`)

- **Disclosure Audit Verdict**: **`FAIL (2 Defects)`**
  1. Snap #1 label placed in the top-left UI collision zone with low contrast.
  2. Snaps #2, #3, #4, and #5 lacked `#إعلان` entirely.

## 3. Human Review & Localization/Technical Edits (`المراجعة البشرية والتعديلات الرقابية والتقنية`)

1. **Added High-Contrast `#إعلان` Pill Across All 5 Snaps**: Placed a clean black-pill white-text `#إعلان` badge in the top-center safe zone (`180px` below the top edge) on all 5 snaps.
2. **Enabled Native Paid Partnership Tag**: Linked the brand's official handle via the native Paid Partnership tool (which also unlocks direct Spark Ads / Branded Content ad boosting in Ads Manager!).

## 4. Final Reviewed Example Outcome (`النتيجة النهائية الجاهزة للتنفيذ`)

- All 5 story frames published with unmistakable `#إعلان` badges and native Paid Partnership tags enabled.

## 5. What Can Go Wrong & Specific Correction / Fallback (`حالة الفشل المحتملة وخطة الإنقاذ والبديل`)

- **Common Failure Mode (`ما الذي قد يسير بشكل خاطئ؟`)**:
  A creator posts a TikTok video promoting a brand's discount code without toggling `Commercial Content Disclosure` ON; TikTok's automated commercial-content detector flags the promo code in the audio/caption and suppresses the video's For You feed distribution.
- **Specific Correction & Non-AI / Platform Fallback (`الإصلاح المحدد والبديل الآمن`)**:
  Remind creators that enabling the native **`Commercial Content / Paid Partnership`** toggle **protects** their account from undisclosed-commercial-content suppression and allows the brand to boost the post with paid media budget.
