## 1. Use It When & Required Inputs (`متى تستخدمها وما المدخلات المطلوبة؟`)

- **Use It When (EN)**: Use when contracting creators across multiple MENA countries (KSA, UAE, Egypt) or flying a creator from one Arab country to film commercial content in another (e.g., flying an Egyptian or Saudi creator to shoot an ad in Dubai or Riyadh).
- **متى تستخدمها (AR)**: <span dir="rtl">تُستخدم عند التعاقد مع مؤثرين من عدة دول عربية أو استضافة مؤثر من دولة لتصوير إعلان تجاري داخل دولة أخرى (مثل سفر مؤثر لتصوير إعلان في دبي أو الرياض).</span>
- **When NOT to Use It (EN)**: Do not fly a non-resident creator into Dubai or Riyadh to shoot paid commercial content on a standard tourist visa without securing the local visiting advertiser / shooting permit through a licensed local agency.
- **متى لا تستخدمها (AR)**: <span dir="rtl">لا تقم باستضافة مؤثر غير مقيم لتصوير إعلان تجاري مدفوع في دبي أو الرياض بتأشيرة سياحية عادية دون استخراج تصريح معلن زائر / تصريح تصوير عبر وكالة محلية مرخصة.</span>

### Exact Inputs to Prepare (`المدخلات المطلوب تجهيزها قبل البدء`)
- Creator Nationality & Country of Residence (`KSA`, `UAE`, `Egypt`, `Other`)
- Physical Shooting Location (`Where the creator will film`) AND Target Ad Audience Location (`Where the ad will run`)
- Creator's existing permits (`KSA Mawthooq`, `UAE Media Council Permit + DED/Freezone License`, `Egypt Tax Card / E-Invoice`)

## 3. Complete End-to-End Worked Example (`مثال تطبيقي متكامل من المدخلات حتى المراجعة البشرية والنتيجة النهائية`)

*(Illustrative scenario — original example for training, never represented as a verified client outcome)*

### Step A: Sample Brief / Input (`موجز المدخلات`)
- **Campaign**: Regional Tourism & Retail App flying 3 creators (1 Saudi, 1 Egyptian, 1 Emirati) to Dubai for a 4-day paid brand shoot, followed by a 3-day shoot in Riyadh.

### Step B: Initial Output (`المخرج الأولي`)
- **Permit Routing Plan**:
  1. **Dubai Leg (`UAE Media Council`)**:
     - Emirati creator: Verified active UAE Media Council Advertiser Permit displayed in bio.
     - Visiting Saudi & Egyptian creators: Registered under the partner Dubai talent agency for **UAE Visiting Advertiser Permits (`تصريح معلن زائر`)** 14 days prior to travel (holding a Saudi `Mawthooq` license does NOT exempt a creator filming ads in Dubai from UAE Media Council rules!).
  2. **Riyadh Leg (`KSA GAMR`)**:
     - Saudi creator: Verified active `Mawthooq` license (`AMP-281`).
     - Visiting Emirati & Egyptian creators: Contracted and cleared directly through the licensed Riyadh advertising agency under GAMR visiting/agency sponsorship rules.

### Step C: Human Review & Edits (`التدقيق البشري والتعديلات`)
1. **Added 14-Day Lead Time Gate**: Blocked flight ticket booking until permit numbers for both legs were issued and logged in the production tracker.
2. **Tax Withholding & E-Invoice Check (Egypt)**: Confirmed the Egyptian creator's contract includes their Egyptian Tax Registration Number (`رقم التسجيل الضريبي`) and electronic invoice (`الفاتورة الإلكترونية`) compliance.

### Step D: Final Reviewed Example Outcome (`النتيجة النهائية المعتمدة`)
- All 3 creators cleared with jurisdiction-specific permits for both the UAE and KSA shoots 10 days before departure, with zero cross-border permit violations.

## 4. What Can Go Wrong & How to Fix It (`الأخطاء الشائعة وخطة الإنقاذ`)

- **Failure Case (`المشكلة المحتملة`)**: A Dubai agency books a famous Saudi creator (who holds a valid KSA `Mawthooq` license) to film paid restaurant & mall ads inside Dubai without obtaining a UAE Media Council Visiting Advertiser Permit, assuming `Mawthooq` is valid across the GCC.
- **Correction & Fallback (`الحل الإجرائي والبديل الآمن`)**: Enforce the **Jurisdiction Independence Rule**: `KSA Mawthooq != UAE Media Council Permit`. Always verify the permit required by (a) the country where the creator is physically filming/residing AND (b) the country where the ad is targeted.
