## 3. The Directly Usable Artifact (`الأداة التطبيقية الجاهزة للنسخ والتنفيذ`)

### Saudi GAMR 'Mawthooq' (`موثوق`) Pre-Contracting Verification Checklist

#### 1. Mandatory 5-Point Mawthooq Gate (Zero Contracts Without 5/5 Pass)
- [ ] **Check 1 — Active `Mawthooq` License Number Collected**:
  - Request the creator's official **GAMR Mawthooq License Number (`رقم ترخيص موثوق لتقديم المحتوى الإعلاني للأفراد عبر منصات التواصل الاجتماعي`)** before sharing campaign briefs or shipping PR boxes that carry a posting expectation.
- [ ] **Check 2 — Expiration & Account Handle Match**:
  - Confirm the license is currently **Active (`ساري`)** (Mawthooq licenses are valid for 3 years; fee SAR 15,000) and that the exact social handle (` Snapchat / TikTok / Instagram / X `) being used for the ad is registered under that license.
- [ ] **Check 3 — Non-Saudi / Cross-Border Creator Rule (`AMP-282`)**:
  - Confirm compliance with GAMR's rules regarding non-Saudi individuals advertising into the Kingdom (non-Saudis cannot advertise independently as individuals without a licensed Saudi advertising agency/entity contract and applicable GAMR/commercial permits).
- [ ] **Check 4 — Sector-Specific Secondary Permit (`SFDA / MOH / Real Estate REGA / FinTech SAMA-CMA`)**:
  - `Mawthooq` is the *media* permit; if the ad promotes a real estate property in KSA, verify the **REGA Fal (`رخصة فال العقارية`)** ad license; if a clinic/health service, verify the **MOH/SFDA** permit (`AMP-070`, `AMP-286`); if financial/investment, verify **CMA/SAMA** authorization.
- [ ] **Check 5 — Mandatory Contractual Representation Clause**:
  - Insert the clause below into every Saudi creator agreement/PO:
  > *"يقر الطرف الثاني (المؤثر/المعلن) بأنه يحمل ترخيص «موثوق» ساري المفعول رقم [________] الصادر عن الهيئة العامة لتنظيم الإعلام (GAMR)، ويتعهد بالمحافظة على سريانه طوال فترة الحملة، مع الالتزام بوضع وسم (#إعلان) بوضوح، والالتزام بكافة ضوابط المحتوى الإعلامي والأنظمة المرعية في المملكة العربية السعودية."*

## 6. Worked Example Summary & Failure Recovery (`ملخص المثال التطبيقي وخطة الإنقاذ`)

*(Full step-by-step worked example with annotated human edits is also provided in `EXAMPLE.md`)*

- **Sample Input Brief**: - **Campaign**: Riyadh Coffee & Roastery Chain launching a new winter menu with 12 Saudi lifestyle & food creators shortlisted by an AI influencer tool (`SAR 180,000` total creator budget).
- **Pre-Flight Audit Result**:
  - `8` creators supplied active verified `Mawthooq` license numbers.
  - `2` micro-influencers had **no `Mawthooq` license** (`"نعلن بدون ترخيص لأن حسابنا صغير"` — illegal under GAMR rules, which apply to any paid/barter ad regardless of follower size).
  - `1` creator supplied a `Mawthooq` license that had **expired 4 months ago**.
  - `1` creator was a tourist on a visit visa filming in Riyadh with no Saudi agency/commercial permit.
- **Human Review & Key Edits Applied**: 1. **Added License Column to Master Campaign Tracker**: Made `mawthooq_license_number` and `mawthooq_expiry_date` mandatory blocking fields in the agency's procurement sheet before finance can issue any 50% advance payment.
2. **Paired with `#إعلان` Disclosure (`AMP-287`)**: Added mandatory `#إعلان` overlay check on all story frames.
- **Final Usable Output**: - `12 / 12` contracted Saudi creators verified with active `GAMR Mawthooq` licenses and documented compliance clauses prior to campaign launch.
- **What Can Go Wrong**: A brand sends 30 'free PR gift boxes' with a written WhatsApp message saying `'الهدية مقابل تصوير 3 سنابات مع رابط المتجر وكود الخصم'`; GAMR treats barter/gifted posts with a posting or promo-code obligation as commercial advertising requiring `Mawthooq` and `#إعلان` disclosure.
- **Correction & Fallback**: Apply the exact same `Mawthooq` verification checklist to **barter, affiliate-code, and PR-gifting campaigns** whenever there is an agreement or expectation to post.
