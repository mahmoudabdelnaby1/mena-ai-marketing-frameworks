# End-to-End Worked Example & Failure Recovery — `FW-040` (`AMP-183`)

- **Framework**: Saudi Intra-Kingdom Regional Persona & Sector Differentiation Aid (Riyadh vs. Jeddah vs. Eastern Province)
- **العنوان بالعربية**: أداة تفصيل الخطاب التسويقي وشخصية المشتري داخل المملكة (الرياض مقابل جدة مقابل المنطقة الشرقية)
- **Selected Artifact Type**: `DECISION_AID`

## 1. Realistic Sample Brief / Input (`المدخلات وموجز الحالة العملية`)

- **Brand**: Saudi Commercial Fleet & Logistics Telematics SaaS (`B2B`).
- **Task**: Create 3 localized landing page & LinkedIn ad variants for Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam/Jubail.

## 2. Initial Generated / Completed Output (`المخرج الأولي عند تطبيق الأداة`)

- **Riyadh Variant (`Enterprise & Last-Mile Distribution`)**: Focused on Riyadh dry-port distribution hubs, cold-chain monitoring in 48°C central summer heat, and integration with Saudi Transport General Authority (`الهيئة العامة للنقل - وصل / بيان`).
- **Jeddah Variant (`Port-to-Warehouse & Hajj/Umrah Fleet`)**: Focused on container drayage from Jeddah Islamic Port, Makkah/Madinah seasonal fleet routing, and coastal humidity sensor telemetry.
- **Dammam/Jubail Variant (`Industrial & Hazmat Safety`)**: Focused on Jubail industrial zone safety compliance, Aramco contractor vehicle standards, and King Fahd Causeway / GCC cross-border fleet tracking.

## 3. Human Review & Localization/Technical Edits (`المراجعة البشرية والتعديلات الرقابية والتقنية`)

1. **Verified Regulatory Integrations**: Confirmed accurate mention of the Saudi Transport General Authority's `Wasl` (`منصة وصل`) integration across all 3 pages.
2. **Checked Shingle Uniqueness (`AMP-012`)**: Confirmed each regional landing page has `<20%` text overlap and cites real regional logistics corridors.

## 4. Final Reviewed Example Outcome (`النتيجة النهائية الجاهزة للتنفيذ`)

- 3 region-specific Saudi B2B campaigns launched with tailored industry hooks for Central, Western, and Eastern Provinces.

## 5. What Can Go Wrong & Specific Correction / Fallback (`حالة الفشل المحتملة وخطة الإنقاذ والبديل`)

- **Common Failure Mode (`ما الذي قد يسير بشكل خاطئ؟`)**:
  A fashion brand runs a 'Heavy Winter Velvet & Fur Kashta Coat' campaign with identical spend in Riyadh (where winter nights drop to 6°C) and Jeddah (where winter temperatures stay 24–29°C), wasting half the Jeddah budget on heavy winter coats.
- **Specific Correction & Non-AI / Platform Fallback (`الإصلاح المحدد والبديل الآمن`)**:
  Always check the **Climate & Seasonality Row** of the Intra-Kingdom Matrix before launching weather-dependent retail or F&B campaigns across Riyadh, Jeddah, Abha/Asir, and Dammam.
