### Step C: Human Review & Edits (`التدقيق البشري والتعديلات`)

## 4. What Can Go Wrong & How to Fix It (`الأخطاء الشائعة وخطة الإنقاذ`)

- **Failure Case (`المشكلة المحتملة`)**: A fashion brand runs a 'Heavy Winter Velvet & Fur Kashta Coat' campaign with identical spend in Riyadh (where winter nights drop to 6°C) and Jeddah (where winter temperatures stay 24–29°C), wasting half the Jeddah budget on heavy winter coats.
- **Correction & Fallback (`الحل الإجرائي والبديل الآمن`)**: Always check the **Climate & Seasonality Row** of the Intra-Kingdom Matrix before launching weather-dependent retail or F&B campaigns across Riyadh, Jeddah, Abha/Asir, and Dammam.
