Use anonymized placeholders (`{first_name}`, `ORD_10045`) during prompt testing and restrict live customer data to zero-data-retention enterprise API endpoints or local deterministic scripts.

## 6. Worked Example Summary & Failure Recovery (`ملخص المثال التطبيقي وخطة الإنقاذ`)

*(Full step-by-step worked example with annotated human edits is also provided in `EXAMPLE.md`)*

- **Sample Input Brief**: - **Brand**: Saudi Commercial Fleet & Logistics Telematics SaaS (`B2B`).
- **Task**: Create 3 localized landing page & LinkedIn ad variants for Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam/Jubail.
- **Human Review & Key Edits Applied**: 1. **Verified Regulatory Integrations**: Confirmed accurate mention of the Saudi Transport General Authority's `Wasl` (`منصة وصل`) integration across all 3 pages.
2. **Checked Shingle Uniqueness (`AMP-012`)**: Confirmed each regional landing page has `<20%` text overlap and cites real regional logistics corridors.
- **Final Usable Output**: - 3 region-specific Saudi B2B campaigns launched with tailored industry hooks for Central, Western, and Eastern Provinces.
- **What Can Go Wrong**: A fashion brand runs a 'Heavy Winter Velvet & Fur Kashta Coat' campaign with identical spend in Riyadh (where winter nights drop to 6°C) and Jeddah (where winter temperatures stay 24–29°C), wasting half the Jeddah budget on heavy winter coats.
- **Correction & Fallback**: Always check the **Climate & Seasonality Row** of the Intra-Kingdom Matrix before launching weather-dependent retail or F&B campaigns across Riyadh, Jeddah, Abha/Asir, and Dammam.
