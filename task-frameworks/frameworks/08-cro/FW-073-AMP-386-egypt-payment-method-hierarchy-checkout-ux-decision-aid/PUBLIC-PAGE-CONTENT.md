# Egypt Payment-Method & Checkout UX Decision Aid
## <span dir="rtl">أداة قرار لترتيب وسائل الدفع وتجربة إتمام الطلب في المتجر المصري</span>

Choose and present payment methods using the merchant's supported providers, current fees, customer evidence, delivery operations, and reconciliation capacity.

## Prepare
Gather the store's own checkout and settled-order data, current enabled methods, provider fees and settlement terms, refund process, and mobile checkout experience.

## Workflow
1. Establish a dated merchant-specific baseline for checkout completion and payment outcomes.
2. Verify supported methods, fees, settlement, refunds, and account requirements with the current provider.
3. Identify friction from session data, support contacts, payment failures, and order-state mismatches.
4. Present clear method names, accurate fees, concise instructions, and accurate payment states.
5. Test one change at a time and monitor completion alongside failed payments, refunds, cancellations, and delivered contribution.
6. Keep or change options based on the merchant's evidence and operational capacity.

## Boundary
Do not infer one national payment mix or required set of methods from secondary benchmarks. Provider options and fees vary; confirm them directly. This is general operating guidance, not financial advice.
