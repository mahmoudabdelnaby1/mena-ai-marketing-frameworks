# Evidence Register & Source Boundaries — `FW-073`

This decision aid uses the merchant's own data and current provider terms; it does not make a national payment-mix claim.

| Evidence source | What to verify |
|---|---|
| Merchant's payment provider | Enabled methods, fees, settlement, refunds, account eligibility, and integration behavior. |
| E-commerce platform documentation | Checkout controls, supported payment states, notifications, and audit trail. |
| Merchant's checkout sessions and settled order records | Baseline completion, payment failure, cancellations, refunds, delivered orders, and support contacts. |

Use recent records with a clearly stated period and consistent metric definitions. Do not treat provider marketing or a secondary-market estimate as the merchant's actual customer mix.
