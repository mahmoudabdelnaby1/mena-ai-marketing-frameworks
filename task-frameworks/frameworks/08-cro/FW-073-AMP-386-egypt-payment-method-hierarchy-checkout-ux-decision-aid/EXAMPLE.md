# FW-073 — Worked Example

**Illustrative scenario only; figures are synthetic and not market benchmarks or client outcomes.** A mobile store sees customers asking whether a transfer is received and whether cash-on-delivery has an additional fee.

The team reviews its own payment and support records, confirms fees and settlement with its provider, rewrites checkout labels to explain the pending state, and tests a clearer payment-method layout. It compares completion, failed payments, customer contacts, refunds, and delivered orders with a dated baseline before deciding whether to keep the change.
