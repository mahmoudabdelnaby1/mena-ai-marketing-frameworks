# Market-Specific Marketing Permission Review
## <span dir="rtl">مراجعة الموافقة التسويقية حسب السوق والقناة</span>

A practical decision aid for documenting audience source, campaign purpose, permission evidence, channel controls, and accountable review before direct marketing.

## Use it when
Before adding a contact source, launching in a new market, changing message purpose, or combining email, SMS, WhatsApp, and paid-media audiences.

## Review sequence
1. Define the recipient market, audience source, purpose, channel, and message type.
2. Record the evidence and scope of permission or notice, including its source and date.
3. Check current primary official requirements for the exact market and channel with qualified local review. Do not infer a rule or legal basis from another jurisdiction.
4. Test opt-out and suppression handling across connected sending systems.
5. Document the decision, required controls, reviewer, source links, unresolved items, and review date. Hold any use with missing required evidence.

## Output
A campaign-specific review record, required controls, and unresolved questions for the accountable reviewer.

## Boundary
This is general operational guidance, not legal advice, a consent template, or a determination that a campaign is lawful.
