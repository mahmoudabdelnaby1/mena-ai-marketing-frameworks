# FW-044 — Market-Specific Marketing Permission Review
## مراجعة الموافقة التسويقية حسب السوق والقناة

### Purpose
A decision aid for organizing a market-specific review before direct marketing. It does not interpret legislation or determine a legal basis.

### Required inputs
Market and recipient location; audience source and collection date; campaign purpose; channel and message type; permission/notice records; suppression history; data controller and vendor; accountable reviewer.

### Decision workflow
1. **Define the use.** State who will receive the communication, why, through which channel, and whether the use matches the original collection context.
2. **Inspect the evidence.** Record the source, wording, date, and scope of any permission or notice. Flag missing or conflicting evidence.
3. **Check the exact market and channel.** Consult current primary official sources and qualified local privacy/legal review. Do not transfer a rule, exception, or legal basis from another jurisdiction.
4. **Test preference controls.** Confirm how objections and opt-outs are captured, synchronized, and respected in every connected sending system.
5. **Make a documented decision.** Record approved audience scope, required controls, reviewer, source links, unresolved items, and review date. Hold the campaign if a required answer is missing.

### Output
A campaign-specific review record and a list of controls and unresolved questions.

### Boundaries
This is not a legal opinion, consent template, or blanket determination for KSA, UAE, Qatar, Egypt, or any other market. Laws and platform rules change; qualified local review is required.
