# `FW-044` — Market-Specific Marketing Permission Review
## مراجعة الموافقة التسويقية حسب السوق والقناة

- **One-Sentence Promise (EN)**: Help a team document the audience, purpose, permission source, channel rules to verify, and reviewer before a direct-marketing campaign.
- **الوعد التطبيقي في جملة واحدة (AR)**: ساعد الفريق على توثيق الجمهور والغرض ومصدر الموافقة والقواعد المطلوب التحقق منها لكل قناة والمراجع قبل حملة تسويق مباشر.
- **Selected Artifact Type**: `DECISION_AID`
- **Intended Audience**: CRM, lifecycle, privacy, and marketing-operations teams.
- **Markets & Languages**: Use separately for each market and channel; obtain qualified local review.

## Use It When
Before adding a contact source, launching direct marketing in a new market, changing a message purpose, or combining email, SMS, WhatsApp, and paid-media audiences.

## Quick Start
1. Record market, audience source, purpose, channel, message type, and permission evidence.
2. Check current official rules and internal policy for that exact market and channel; do not infer consent requirements from another jurisdiction.
3. Record the decision, required opt-out/suppression handling, reviewer, and re-check date before launch.

This decision aid is not legal advice and does not determine whether a campaign is lawful.
