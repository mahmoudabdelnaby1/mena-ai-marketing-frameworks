# FW-044 — Worked Example

**Illustrative workflow only; not a legal determination.** A retailer plans to reuse event leads in email and WhatsApp across two markets.

- The CRM owner documents how each list was collected, the notice shown at collection, the original purpose, and any existing suppression records.
- The team separates records by market and intended channel instead of treating one permission record as universal.
- A qualified reviewer checks current official requirements for each market and channel and lists any missing evidence.
- The team records the decision, required opt-out handling, and campaign owner. Records with unresolved permission scope are held out until reviewed.

No statute, legal basis, or compliance conclusion is inferred by this example.
