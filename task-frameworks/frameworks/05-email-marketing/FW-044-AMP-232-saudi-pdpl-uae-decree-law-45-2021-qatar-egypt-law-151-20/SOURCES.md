# Evidence Register & Source Boundaries — `FW-044`

This decision aid makes no legal conclusion. The reviewer must consult current primary sources for the specific market, channel, purpose, and audience.

| Source category | Use | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Official data-protection authority and communications-regulator sources for the recipient's market | Verify current rules and regulator guidance relevant to the intended use. | No single source or jurisdiction is a substitute for review of the exact facts and channel. |
| Current platform policy and contract terms | Verify platform-specific messaging, advertising, and audience-use restrictions. | Platform permission does not establish legal permission. |
| Internal collection, notice, and suppression records | Establish what the organization can document about the audience and prior preferences. | Missing evidence must be recorded; do not assume permission. |

Obtain qualified local privacy/legal review before sending where the rule or permission scope is uncertain.
