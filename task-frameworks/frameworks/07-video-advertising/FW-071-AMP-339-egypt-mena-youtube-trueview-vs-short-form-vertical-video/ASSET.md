#### The "5-Second Self-Qualifying Hook" Rule for Skippable YouTube TrueView (`Zero-Cost Disqualification`)
- In Seconds `0.0s – 4.8s` (before the `Skip Ad` button unlocks at `5.0s`), state BOTH:
  1. **The Brand Name & Visual Product**, AND
  2. **Who the ad is NOT for / Who it IS for** (e.g., `"لو بتدير شركة فيها أكتر من 20 موظف في مصر... الفيديو ده ليك"`).
  - *Result*: Teenagers and non-ICP viewers click `Skip` at Second 5 (**you pay `0.00` for their 5-second brand impression!**), and you only pay CPV when a real business owner watches past 30 seconds or clicks your link!

## 6. Worked Example Summary & Failure Recovery (`ملخص المثال التطبيقي وخطة الإنقاذ`)

*(Full step-by-step worked example with annotated human edits is also provided in `EXAMPLE.md`)*

- **Sample Input Brief**: - **Advertiser**: Cairo & New Administrative Capital Commercial Real Estate Developer (`Minimum Down Payment: 850,000 EGP`).
- **Previous Mistake**: Spent `100%` of video budget on TikTok and Facebook broad reels with a mass entertainment hook; paid for millions of impressions among 18–22 year-old students and generated `90%` unqualified leads.
- **Human Review & Key Edits Applied**: 1. **Disabled Television Game App Placements & Kids Channels**: Added account-level placement exclusions for kids' channels (`YouTube Kids / Nursery Rhymes`) and mobile gaming apps (`AMP-069`) so parents handing iPads to toddlers don't rack up accidental 30-second views!
2. **Supplied Both `16:9` and `9:16` Cuts**: Uploaded native `1920x1080` (`16:9`) for desktop/Smart TV and `1080x1920` (`9:16`) for YouTube Shorts.
- **Final Usable Output**: - Cost per Qualified Investor Lead (`SQL`) dropped by `48%`, while gaining `1.8M` free `<5s` skippable brand impressions among Egyptian desktop and Smart TV viewers.
- **What Can Go Wrong**: A media buyer runs a YouTube TrueView campaign in Egypt or KSA without excluding Children's Music / Cartoon channels (`قنوات أناشيد وأفلام كرتون الأطفال`); toddlers watching cartoons on autoplay let every 45-second ad play to completion without skipping, burning 60% of the budget on toddlers!
- **Correction & Fallback**: Before launching **any** YouTube campaign in MENA, apply the **Mandatory Family/Kids Content Exclusion Checklist**: exclude `Content Suitability -> Embedded YouTube Videos`, exclude `Mobile App Categories`, and attach a negative channel/topic placement list blocking Kids/Cartoon/Nursery Rhyme channels.
