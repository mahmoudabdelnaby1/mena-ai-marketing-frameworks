#### The "5-Second Self-Qualifying Hook" Rule for Skippable YouTube TrueView (`Zero-Cost Disqualification`)
- In Seconds `0.0s – 4.8s` (before the `Skip Ad` button unlocks at `5.0s`), state BOTH:
  1. **The Brand Name & Visual Product**, AND
  2. **Who the ad is NOT for / Who it IS for** (e.g., `"لو بتدير شركة فيها أكتر من 20 موظف في مصر... الفيديو ده ليك"`).
  - *Result*: Teenagers and non-ICP viewers click `Skip` at Second 5 (**you pay `0.00` for their 5-second brand impression!**), and you only pay CPV when a real business owner watches past 30 seconds or clicks your link!

## 3. Complete End-to-End Worked Example (`مثال تطبيقي متكامل من المدخلات حتى المراجعة البشرية والنتيجة النهائية`)

*(Illustrative scenario — original example for training, never represented as a verified client outcome)*

### Step A: Sample Brief / Input (`موجز المدخلات`)
- **Advertiser**: Cairo & New Administrative Capital Commercial Real Estate Developer (`Minimum Down Payment: 850,000 EGP`).
- **Previous Mistake**: Spent `100%` of video budget on TikTok and Facebook broad reels with a mass entertainment hook; paid for millions of impressions among 18–22 year-old students and generated `90%` unqualified leads.

### Step B: Initial Output (`المخرج الأولي`)
- **Reallocated Video Mix (`40% YouTube TrueView In-Stream + Demand Gen` / `40% Meta` / `20% LinkedIn`)**:
  - Scripted a **38-second Skippable YouTube TrueView Ad** where Seconds `0–5` opened on-camera with:
    `"للمستثمرين وأصحاب الشركات اللي بيبحثوا عن مقر إداري في الحي المالي بالعاصمة الإدارية باستلام فوري وعائد إيجاري بعقد موثق..."`
  - Viewers outside the investor segment skipped at Second 5 (`cost = 0 EGP`), while high-net-worth investors watched all 38 seconds (`CPV: 0.45 EGP`) and clicked through to the brochure download.

### Step C: Human Review & Edits (`التدقيق البشري والتعديلات`)
1. **Disabled Television Game App Placements & Kids Channels**: Added account-level placement exclusions for kids' channels (`YouTube Kids / Nursery Rhymes`) and mobile gaming apps (`AMP-069`) so parents handing iPads to toddlers don't rack up accidental 30-second views!
2. **Supplied Both `16:9` and `9:16` Cuts**: Uploaded native `1920x1080` (`16:9`) for desktop/Smart TV and `1080x1920` (`9:16`) for YouTube Shorts.

### Step D: Final Reviewed Example Outcome (`النتيجة النهائية المعتمدة`)
- Cost per Qualified Investor Lead (`SQL`) dropped by `48%`, while gaining `1.8M` free `<5s` skippable brand impressions among Egyptian desktop and Smart TV viewers.

## 4. What Can Go Wrong & How to Fix It (`الأخطاء الشائعة وخطة الإنقاذ`)

- **Failure Case (`المشكلة المحتملة`)**: A media buyer runs a YouTube TrueView campaign in Egypt or KSA without excluding Children's Music / Cartoon channels (`قنوات أناشيد وأفلام كرتون الأطفال`); toddlers watching cartoons on autoplay let every 45-second ad play to completion without skipping, burning 60% of the budget on toddlers!
- **Correction & Fallback (`الحل الإجرائي والبديل الآمن`)**: Before launching **any** YouTube campaign in MENA, apply the **Mandatory Family/Kids Content Exclusion Checklist**: exclude `Content Suitability -> Embedded YouTube Videos`, exclude `Mobile App Categories`, and attach a negative channel/topic placement list blocking Kids/Cartoon/Nursery Rhyme channels.

## 6. How to Measure Success (`خطة القياس والضوابط`)

- **Primary Metric**: Effective Cost per Qualified View (`>=30s` by target demographic), Earned Free Impressions (`Skipped <30s`), and Cost per CRM-Qualified Lead.
- **Baseline**: Prior Meta/TikTok-only video Cost per Qualified Lead.
- **Observation Window**: 21–28 days.
- **Interpretation Boundary**: TrueView CPV is highly efficient for high-consideration storytelling and self-qualifying hooks, while impulse <500 EGP products still rely heavily on Meta/TikTok catalog loops.
