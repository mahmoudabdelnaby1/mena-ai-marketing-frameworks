# End-to-End Worked Example & Failure Recovery — `FW-071` (`AMP-339`)

- **Framework**: Egypt & MENA YouTube TrueView vs. Short-Form Vertical Video Budget Allocation Decision Aid
- **العنوان بالعربية**: أداة قرار موازنة فيديو العلامة التجارية بين `YouTube TrueView` والفيديو القصير في السوق المصري والإقليمي
- **Selected Artifact Type**: `DECISION_AID`
- **Disclaimer**: *All scenarios, brand names, SKU codes, and metrics below are original, realistic illustrative examples for MENA practitioner training and are never represented as verified client outcomes.*

## 1. Realistic Sample Brief / Input (`المدخلات وموجز الحالة العملية`)

- **Advertiser**: Cairo & New Administrative Capital Commercial Real Estate Developer (`Minimum Down Payment: 850,000 EGP`).
- **Previous Mistake**: Spent `100%` of video budget on TikTok and Facebook broad reels with a mass entertainment hook; paid for millions of impressions among 18–22 year-old students and generated `90%` unqualified leads.

## 2. Initial Generated / Completed Output (`المخرج الأولي عند تطبيق الأداة`)

- **Reallocated Video Mix (`40% YouTube TrueView In-Stream + Demand Gen` / `40% Meta` / `20% LinkedIn`)**:
  - Scripted a **38-second Skippable YouTube TrueView Ad** where Seconds `0–5` opened on-camera with:
    `"للمستثمرين وأصحاب الشركات اللي بيبحثوا عن مقر إداري في الحي المالي بالعاصمة الإدارية باستلام فوري وعائد إيجاري بعقد موثق..."`
  - Viewers outside the investor segment skipped at Second 5 (`cost = 0 EGP`), while high-net-worth investors watched all 38 seconds (`CPV: 0.45 EGP`) and clicked through to the brochure download.

## 3. Human Review & Localization/Technical Edits (`المراجعة البشرية والتعديلات الرقابية والتقنية`)

1. **Disabled Television Game App Placements & Kids Channels**: Added account-level placement exclusions for kids' channels (`YouTube Kids / Nursery Rhymes`) and mobile gaming apps (`AMP-069`) so parents handing iPads to toddlers don't rack up accidental 30-second views!
2. **Supplied Both `16:9` and `9:16` Cuts**: Uploaded native `1920x1080` (`16:9`) for desktop/Smart TV and `1080x1920` (`9:16`) for YouTube Shorts.

## 4. Final Reviewed Example Outcome (`النتيجة النهائية الجاهزة للتنفيذ`)

- Cost per Qualified Investor Lead (`SQL`) dropped by `48%`, while gaining `1.8M` free `<5s` skippable brand impressions among Egyptian desktop and Smart TV viewers.

## 5. What Can Go Wrong & Specific Correction / Fallback (`حالة الفشل المحتملة وخطة الإنقاذ والبديل`)

- **Common Failure Mode (`ما الذي قد يسير بشكل خاطئ؟`)**:
  A media buyer runs a YouTube TrueView campaign in Egypt or KSA without excluding Children's Music / Cartoon channels (`قنوات أناشيد وأفلام كرتون الأطفال`); toddlers watching cartoons on autoplay let every 45-second ad play to completion without skipping, burning 60% of the budget on toddlers!
- **Specific Correction & Non-AI / Platform Fallback (`الإصلاح المحدد والبديل الآمن`)**:
  Before launching **any** YouTube campaign in MENA, apply the **Mandatory Family/Kids Content Exclusion Checklist**: exclude `Content Suitability -> Embedded YouTube Videos`, exclude `Mobile App Categories`, and attach a negative channel/topic placement list blocking Kids/Cartoon/Nursery Rhyme channels.
