# Digital Marketing Task Frameworks

A practical library of **92 independent, task-specific frameworks** across nine digital marketing disciplines. The collection turns defined marketing jobs into usable workflows, checklists, decision aids, operating guides, prompt templates, and playbooks. It is documentation for human teams, not a hosted service or executable agent.

The nine disciplines are SEO (12 frameworks), PPC (10), social media (10), content (10), email (10), influencer marketing (10), video advertising (10), conversion optimization (10), and marketing automation (10). The catalog is the source of truth for the published inventory.

## How to use a framework

1. Choose the framework that matches the task.
2. Supply the real business context and required inputs.
3. Follow the workflow and adapt the output to the channel, market, and audience.
4. Verify changing platform and market requirements against current primary sources.
5. Have an accountable person review the work before it is used.

## Boundaries

These materials are general working aids, not legal, financial, privacy, platform-policy, or compliance determinations. Examples may be synthetic and must not be presented as client results. They do not guarantee campaign performance, search rankings, indexing, or AI citations. Validate current requirements and assign a qualified human reviewer before acting.
