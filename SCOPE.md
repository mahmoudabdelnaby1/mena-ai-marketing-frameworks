# Scope and editorial decisions

## Why six

The supplied runs contain multiple overlapping framework builds, exploratory research, examples, and competing handoff/build reports. The handoff records themselves state that some selections are recommendations rather than authorization and that evidence coverage is incomplete. The portfolio already has specialized playbooks for Arabic copy localization, AEO/GEO review, ad preflight, local SEO, WhatsApp commerce, event measurement, and incrementality. Publishing every draft as a separate item would create duplication and expose unsupported claims.

The six frameworks at the repository root are a curated, non-exhaustive cross-functional operating collection. The separate `task-frameworks/` library contains 92 specialized task-level assets across nine disciplines. These two layers serve different jobs and should be linked clearly rather than collapsed into one count. The six core frameworks consolidate related drafts while preserving distinct jobs:

1. Evidence review for factual marketing content.
2. End-to-end Arabic AI creative localization and QA.
3. Rights and disclosure handling for creators and synthetic media.
4. Security and service boundaries for customer-facing conversation AI.
5. Privacy-aware handling of customer data in AI workflows.
6. Governance for AI recommendations and marketing measurement.

## Merge and exclusion rules

- Search discoverability and content indexing details point to the existing SEO/AEO/GEO playbooks; this collection does not claim a special ranking or AI-citation mechanism.
- Region-specific advertising permits, product-sector rules, WhatsApp opt-in and delivery details, event implementation, and controlled-lift test design remain in their dedicated playbooks and current official sources.
- Country-specific legal interpretations, precise penalties, changing platform thresholds, provider availability, and numerical market claims were excluded unless independently substantiated for the actual scope.
- No research volume, pattern count, score, pass-rate, or market-demand claim is repeated from generated build reports as if independently verified.
- The original folder remains the research/archive source. This repository contains the edited public deliverables only.

## Change control

When changing a framework, preserve the English/Arabic pair, update `catalog.json`, check its source links, and review the corresponding portfolio pages and sitemap. A new framework should add a distinct operational job and have a clear boundary from existing playbooks before publication.
