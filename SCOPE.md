# Scope and editorial decisions

## Current collection

This repository publishes 92 standalone, task-specific digital marketing frameworks across nine disciplines. Each item addresses a defined job and is listed in `task-frameworks/FRAMEWORK-CATALOG.json` and the CSV catalog.

## Selection and overlap rules

- Keep each deliverable tied to a distinct task, decision, or workflow.
- Consolidate overlapping drafts rather than publishing near-duplicates.
- Use existing specialized playbooks for narrower implementation guidance; do not maintain redundant copies here.
- Validate changing platform behavior and market requirements against current primary sources.
- Exclude unsupported legal interpretations, precise penalties, changing platform thresholds, and numerical market claims unless substantiated for the actual scope.
- Do not present source-screening volume, scores, pass rates, or market demand as independently verified outcomes.
- The original research folders remain separate; this repository contains the selected public deliverables.

## Change control

When changing a framework, preserve its available language pair, update the task catalog, check its source links, and review the corresponding portfolio page and sitemap. A new item must add a distinct task and have a clear boundary from existing frameworks, skills, and playbooks before publication.
