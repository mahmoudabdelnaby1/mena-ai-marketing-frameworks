## Evidence boundary

Platform documentation describes the platform’s own product or policy. It does not independently validate business outcomes. NIST frameworks are voluntary risk-management references, not certifications. C2PA provenance metadata is not a truth guarantee. FTC guidance applies to the U.S. context; each other market requires its own current primary-source review. Nothing here substitutes for qualified legal, privacy, security, or platform review.
