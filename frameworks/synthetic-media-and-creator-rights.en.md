# Creator, UGC & synthetic media rights

Keep creator and AI-assisted assets traceable: document permissions, disclose material relationships where required, preserve edits, and review the final placement.

## When to use

Use for creator briefs, testimonials, UGC, synthetic or altered likenesses, cloned or generated voices, and reuse of creator assets in paid media.

## Inputs

- Asset provenance and identity of the creator, performer, or rights holder
- Written permissions and intended channels, markets, duration, edits, and paid usage
- Disclosure requirements supplied by platform and qualified local reviewers

## Workflow

1. **Inventory the asset.** Label original, edited, generated, or mixed media; record who supplied each source element.
2. **Map permission to use.** Check the permission actually covers likeness, voice, music, location, edits, paid distribution, markets, and time period.
3. **Protect testimonial truth.** Do not invent experience, results, or endorsements. Keep the creator’s meaning intact and retain evidence for material claims.
4. **Check disclosure.** Identify commercial relationships and synthetic alterations that may need disclosure; verify current rules for each platform and market.
5. **Review the final placement.** Review the rendered post or ad, caption, disclosure visibility, crop, and links—not only the source file.
6. **Keep a rights record.** Store permission reference, allowed uses, expiry/review date, versions, approvals, and takedown contact.

## Expected output

> An asset provenance and rights register, disclosure decision, approved final placement, and a clear expiry/takedown path.

## Boundaries and human review

> This is an operational checklist, not a determination of legal rights or licensing. Obtain advice for the relevant jurisdiction and contract.

## Relationship to existing work

> Complements campaign compliance review by focusing on asset provenance, creator permissions, truthful endorsements, and reuse rights.
