# Arabic AI creative localization & QA

Coordinate Arabic copy, dialect, visual generation, and asset review without treating the region as one audience or trusting generated Arabic text inside artwork.

## When to use

Use when adapting a campaign or generating creative for Arabic-speaking audiences across different markets, dialects, formats, or placements.

## Inputs

- Market, audience, placement, objective, offer, and brand voice
- Approved product facts, visual references, usage rights, and prohibited elements
- Required Arabic variety and a reviewer who understands the target audience

## Workflow

1. **Separate the market brief.** Specify country, audience, dialect or register, platform, and placement; do not infer dialect from nationality alone.
2. **Lock the message.** Approve the offer, product facts, CTA, and claims in editable text before generating visuals.
3. **Create variants with controls.** Keep a source brief and version log; generate scene and composition separately from final text when the tool renders Arabic unreliably.
4. **Review Arabic as language.** Check spelling, punctuation, RTL order, diacritics where needed, mixed-script terms, and whether phrasing sounds natural to local reviewers.
5. **Review the image as an ad.** Inspect product fidelity, hands, logos, labels, cultural cues, contrast, safe areas, crop behavior, and mobile legibility.
6. **Clear rights and disclosure.** Confirm permission for likeness, voice, trademarks, and supplied assets; check current platform disclosure rules for the specific placement.
7. **Export and learn.** Name files by market/channel/version, record reviewer and disposition, then compare approved variants using an agreed outcome measure.

## Expected output

> A market-specific creative brief, editable copy, reviewed assets, rights/disclosure notes, and a versioned approval record.

## Boundaries and human review

> Generated visuals can contain plausible-looking errors. Human review is required; platform rules and disclosure requirements change.

## Relationship to existing work

> Extends the existing Arabic Copy Localizer from text adaptation into an end-to-end creative production and QA workflow.
