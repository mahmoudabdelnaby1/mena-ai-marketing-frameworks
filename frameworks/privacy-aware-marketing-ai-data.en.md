# Privacy-aware marketing AI & customer data

Reduce exposure before using customer or CRM data with AI: classify, minimize, control access, document purpose, and verify vendor handling.

## When to use

Use before using customer records, support transcripts, CRM exports, lead lists, or behavioral data in an AI tool or automated marketing workflow.

## Inputs

- Data fields, source, purpose, audience, and intended model/vendor
- Access roles, retention/deletion controls, transfer locations, and contract terms available
- Applicable internal policy and review contact for the relevant market

## Workflow

1. **Map the flow.** Record where the data originates, where it moves, who can access it, and what decisions use the output.
2. **Minimize before transfer.** Remove fields not needed for the task; avoid direct identifiers and sensitive details unless explicitly authorized and necessary.
3. **Check the tool and terms.** Verify retention, training use, access, deletion, subprocessors, region, and contractual controls from current vendor documentation.
4. **Choose a safer method.** Use synthetic or aggregated examples where possible; otherwise use approved environments, role controls, and documented safeguards.
5. **Review output use.** Check whether profiling, targeting, or automated decisions introduce additional review or notice requirements.
6. **Record and govern.** Log purpose, data categories, reviewer, vendor, retention, incident contact, and review date; test deletion and revoke access when no longer needed.

## Expected output

> A data-flow record, minimum-field dataset, vendor and purpose review, access/retention controls, and documented approval decision.

## Boundaries and human review

> This framework does not determine legal basis or compliance. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, data, purpose, and vendor; get qualified privacy/legal review.

## Relationship to existing work

> This is a data-handling framework, not a CRM integration or legal-compliance promise; it complements existing measurement and messaging playbooks.
